Modern Classic app icon (variant 1), selected by the maintainer 2026-10-06:
Generated using OpenAI image_gen on 2026-10-05, with the original blue globe
and branching transfer arrows as the reference. It is distributed under the
same LGPL version 3 terms as the original artwork; retain the attribution above.
This provenance note is not a claim of exclusive artistic authorship.

The exact approved master is modern-classic-master.png. The generated PNGs,
eiskaltdcpp-borderless.icns, eiskaltdcpp.ico and Qt app-brand PNGs for all five
legacy theme directories are recorded by SHA-256 in modern-classic-manifest.json.
The other legacy artwork and non-brand theme icons are retained. Modern toolbar
themes continue to use the Apex-brand resource paths, now containing the approved
app icon.

Repackage without retouching, using macOS sips and iconutil:
  python3 tools/app-icon/generate.py --output /path/to/disposable/staging
Verify formats and current-tool byte reproduction:
  python3 tests/macos/bundle/test_app_icon.py
Current-tool reproduction does not establish cross-version tool reproducibility.
