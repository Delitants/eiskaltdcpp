# EiskaltDC++ 3

EiskaltDC++ combines Direct Connect hub chat, search and TTH sharing with an
optional BitTorrent engine in its native Qt interface. The 3.x line is under
active development; `v3.0.0-pre.1` is being prepared.

- [Getting started](Getting-Started.md)
- [Torrents and DC sharing](Torrents.md)
- [Proxy routing and TLS](Proxy-and-TLS.md)
- [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting.md)
- [Development status](Development-Status.md)

[Published releases](https://github.com/Delitants/eiskaltdcpp/releases) are distinct
from development builds. Only share files you are authorized to distribute.
